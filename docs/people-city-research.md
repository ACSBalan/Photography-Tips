## Accuracy decisions

- Main camera setups use explicit Manual or Manual + Auto ISO. Auto ISO values are expected starting points, not simultaneously fixed settings.
- All shutter values are seconds. Focal lengths are full-frame reference actual values; matching field of view is converted by the interface rather than changing exposure solely for sensor format.
- Motion blur, camera vibration and missed focus are separate diagnoses. For example, a sharp background with smeared hands needs a faster shutter, not a tripod.
- Flash scenes explicitly require flash-power/TTL adjustment. Within ordinary sync operation, changing shutter primarily alters ambient exposure; it is not an equivalent substitute for flash output. Actual flash sync limits remain camera-specific.
- Slow-sync flash can produce both a sharp flash image and an ambient ghost. Rear sync changes trail order, not the existence of ambient motion blur.
- LED flicker has no universal corrective speed. Mechanical shutter, supported anti-flicker/variable-shutter settings and tested exposure times are conditional remedies.
- Phone guidance distinguishes automatic/fixed controls from optional manual apps. A Night-mode capture duration is not assumed to equal one uninterrupted exposure. Phone studio flash, rear-sync and true light trails are explicitly capability-dependent.
- Window reflections are solved with lighting/viewpoint changes, not simply exposure compensation. Aircraft lens contact can transmit vibration. Polarizers can expose stress colours in plastic/laminated panes.
- Underwater guidance requires an appropriately rated housing, seal preparation and competence for the conditions. Colour lost through water is not promised recoverable with white balance.
