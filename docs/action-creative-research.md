# Action, wildlife, close-up and creative content review

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Content file: `src/data/action-creative.json`.

## Coverage

- Action and sport: 10 distinct situations.
- Animals and wildlife: 10 distinct situations.
- Close-up and still life: 10 distinct situations.
- Creative and multi-image techniques: 10 distinct situations.

Every sheet contains original field instructions, a coherent manual or manual-plus-Auto-ISO setup, capability-aware phone guidance, 3 practical reasons, 6 shooting steps, an essential/optional equipment list, 4 scene-specific problem/cause/correction entries, 2 concrete alternatives, a specific tip, safety advice where relevant, source links, related IDs and an original educational diagram specification.

The numerical recipes are original test starting points for the stated light and effect. Source links support the techniques; they are not claims that a manufacturer published these exact combinations, nor capture metadata for an image. Sources were retrieved from manufacturer, primary software documentation and the original zoo/photographer teaching source. No reference photographs or third-party artwork were copied.

## Technical decisions and limits

- M + Auto ISO means aperture and shutter are chosen, ISO is metered automatically within a stated range. The numeric ISO stored with such a scene is an illustrative expected level, not a promise of the selected automatic value.
- Motion scenes preserve the action-stopping shutter when a slower lens requires more ISO. The sheets distinguish moving-subject blur, whole-frame camera shake and focus on the wrong object.
- Panning, ICM, zoom bursts, light painting and timelapse depend on shutter duration. In bright light, an ND filter or different light level may be necessary; increasing aperture numbers indefinitely is not a complete solution.
- Perched insects with flash require a shutter at or below the camera’s normal flash-sync limit. Low-power flash duration can freeze motion only when flash dominates over ambient. High-speed sync is not silently substituted.
- The creative stroboscopic sheet is distinct from event slow-sync flash: a controlled object is recorded in several positions by programmable repeated pulses inside one exposure. The example is 8 flashes at 4 Hz, requiring a 2 s window. Supported pulse limits and thermal guidance must be checked for the flash. Native phone flash is not assumed to support programmable repetition.
- Macro focus is specified at the relevant eye, rim, facet or petal plane. More depth may require alignment or stacking, not automatically f/22.
- Timelapse uses a 3 s **start-to-start** interval with a 1 s exposure as an initial cloud setup. The selected camera’s interval definition must be checked. The 24 fps output rate does not prescribe a 1/48 s capture exposure: temporal sampling is governed by the capture interval and the movement in the scene.
- HDR brackets hold aperture, ISO, focus and balance fixed while changing shutter; both bracket extremes are inspected. Moving objects can ghost.
- Panorama instructions lock exposure and balance, use 30–40% overlap and identify near-object parallax. A polarizer may produce an uneven sky across a wide sweep.
- Focus stacking requires overlapping sharp slices, static subject/light and inspection of merged edge halos. Lightroom does not become a native pixel-level focus stacker merely by importing a sequence: suitable stacking software or supported in-camera stacking is explicitly required.
- Double-exposure modes differ. Average/auto-gain, additive, lighten and darken are not interchangeable. The one-stop reduction for two equal additive contributions is a starting mathematical compensation, not a universal guarantee for different subjects.
- Phone exposure and feature availability vary by model and app. Most apertures are fixed by the lens; macro may use another physical camera. Burst mode does not itself guarantee a fast shutter. Night stacking can ghost movement. Phone pinch zoom is not treated as a continuous optical zoom burst.
- Wildlife and sport safety instructions preserve barriers and distances. No scene requires chasing, baiting, chilling or provoking animals.

## Validation performed

Local data checks found 40 unique scene IDs; exactly 10 in each assigned category; all related IDs resolve within this file; 3–6 reasons, 4–8 steps, 4–6 corrections and 2–3 alternatives per sheet; Auto ISO flags match their mode; selected numeric ISO, aperture and shutter lie inside the stored ranges; focal starts lie inside focal ranges; and all three diagram annotations are at most 65 characters each.

These checks verify content structure and numeric consistency. They do not claim a particular camera/lens/scene was physically field-tested.
