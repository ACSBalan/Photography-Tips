# Nature content research and QA

Reviewed 30 October 2026. The 40 authored recipes are original practical starting points; supporting references explain the technique and safety basis and are not claims that the source supplied the exact recipe.

- Water and motion: 8

## Photographic decisions

- All baseline recipes use Manual exposure, with fixed ISO, aperture and shutter explicitly represented. This avoids hidden aperture-priority or automatic-shutter assumptions. ISO/aperture/shutter ranges describe useful adjustment territory, not guarantees that every combination works.
- Focal lengths are full-frame reference values for matching field of view. The interface is responsible for showing actual lenses on other formats; these recipes do not prescribe arbitrary exposure changes solely for sensor format.
- Pinpoint-star setups require magnified manual focusing and enlarged recorded-frame checks. Their numerical times are starting points. The separate NPF estimator must use actual focal length and actual pixel pitch and must not apply crop factor twice.
- Star-trail capture separates individual-frame exposure from total sequence duration and describes gaps caused by long-exposure dark frames.
- Lunar detail, earthshine, lunar totality and a Moon within a landscape are separate because their brightness, dynamic range and exposure goals differ materially.
- Aurora shutter time follows the changing curtains, not only stellar motion.
- Noctilucent-cloud visibility is tied to local summer, suitable latitude and twilight, with no northern-hemisphere-only prescription.
- Waterfall, textured current, frozen fountain spray, breaking waves and long seascapes explicitly use different motion goals. ND requirements are metered, not invented fixed filter densities.
- Phone entries identify fixed aperture, app-dependent manual controls, optical versus digital reach and the distinction between a computational stack and one continuous exposure.

## Safety decisions

- Partial/annular solar photography requires an intact suitable purpose-made filter over the front objective before aiming. Filter transmission varies; the numerical shutter is a test only. Eclipse glasses at the eye and photographic ND filters are not substitutes. Unfiltered totality is outside this sheet.
- The lightning sheet requires a pre-positioned remote setup and a photographer within substantial enclosed shelter, away from windows, doors, wiring and plumbing. It does not instruct the photographer to stand in an exposed opening. Retrieval waits until safe conditions, with NOAA's 30-minute-after-last-thunder guidance.
- Coastal sheets include relevant tide, swell, stable-ground and safe-route precautions.

## Validation performed

All 40 records have unique IDs, at least four tags, 3–6 reasons, 4–8 shooting steps, four distinct troubleshooting entries, two concrete alternatives, populated phone guidance and scene-specific visual annotations. All related links resolve within this file. Every starting ISO, aperture and shutter is inside its declared range. Each visual has three annotations of at most 65 characters and two comparison labels. Schema validation by the application remains the integration gate.
